## Which course sits in which period (2026-27)

Read from the syllabi in the `bookSHelf` repo (`projects/<course>/Syllabus - * 2026-2027.md`),
each of which states its own periods under "Courses and Time".

| Period | Course | Block |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 | (prep) | — |
| 3, 4 | Introduction to Statistics | 3-or-4 — Stats occupies BOTH halves, so it meets every day in this block, not alternating with another course |
| 5 | Introduction to Programming Concepts and Methodologies (CS) | 5-or-6, odd rotation day |
| 6 | Integrated Math 1 | 5-or-6, even rotation day |
| 7 | Introduction to Statistics | 7-or-8 |
| 8 | — not covered by any syllabus found; out of scope per Steve, 2026-08-21 | 7-or-8 |

Consequence for due-date TIME-of-day: the 3-or-4 block only ever means Stats, so there's no
cross-course collision to worry about there. The 5-or-6 block genuinely alternates between two
different courses (CS / IM1) by rotation day — same clock time, different course, different date.
All three syllabi state the same student-facing rule verbatim: "Assignments are always due at the
start of the class period on the due date," which is exactly the block's period-start time above —
confirmed, no conflict between what students are told and this schedule.
